Lead Ingestion & Webhooks

Manage public lead creation keys, webhook endpoints, and dynamic field mappings into Nrtur's canonical 8-field Lead model.

Nrtur Core Architecture Rule: Single name Field for Leads

In Nrtur, Contacts have first_name and last_name, but Leads accept a single name field (dto.PublicCreateLeadRequest.name). External services (Shopify, Meta) that send separate first and last names must be mapped/concatenated into Nrtur's single name field before CreateViaLeadAPI is executed.
